Varam Privacy Policy

Version 1 — effective 5 November 2026 · last updated 6 November 2026

Varam is a story app for children. Parents set it up; children read stories, answer quizzes and learn along the way. This policy explains, in plain words, what information Varam keeps, why, who else sees it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it.

In this policy, "Varam", "we" and "us" mean the people who run the Varam app. "You" means the parent or guardian who signs in. "Your child" means the child who uses Varam on your account.

1. The short version

  • A parent signs in and agrees to this policy before a child profile is made.
  • We keep only what Varam needs to work: your sign-in, your child's profile, and their reading.
  • We do not show ads, track your child across apps, sell data or build profiles for marketing.
  • We do not collect your child's location, contacts, photos or phone number.
  • You can see, correct or delete everything at any time in Parent Settings.
  • Questions? Write to Varam Support at varam.app@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days.

If you or your child send feedback in the app, we keep the type of feedback, any rating or reaction, the message you write, the screen it was sent from and the app version. Please do not put personal details in the message.

What we do not collect

Location, contacts, photos, videos, phone number, advertising ID, browsing history, or anything from other apps. Varam has no advertising, no analytics tools and no crash-reporting tools.

3. Voice search

Voice search is off until a parent turns it on in Parent Settings → Privacy & data controls. While it is off, the microphone button does not appear and Varam never asks to use the microphone.

When it is on and your child taps the microphone in Search, your phone's speech recognition turns their voice into words. On most Android phones this is Google's service, which may send the voice to Google to do this (Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy). Varam only receives the words, uses them to find stories, and does not keep them. Android asks your permission before the microphone is used for the first time.

Your phone's keyboard may have its own microphone key (for example on Gboard). That key belongs to the keyboard, not to Varam, so the Varam switch cannot hide it. If your child uses it, the keyboard's own speech service hears their voice and types the words in, under the keyboard maker's terms.

4. What stays on the phone

The phone keeps a copy of your child's profile and reading progress so Varam opens quickly. Reading settings (narration on or off, speed, text size, read-along, light or dark) are kept only on the phone and are not sent to Varam. Your sign-in and any downloaded stories are stored encrypted, with a key kept in the phone's secure storage. Varam's data is left out of phone backups. Uninstalling Varam removes all of it from the phone.

5. Who else handles the information

We use a few services to run Varam. They handle information only to provide their service to us.

Some of these are outside India. We do not send information to any country the Government of India has restricted.

We do not sell, rent or share information about you or your child with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

6. How long we keep it

What How long
Your account, your child's profile, reading progress, saved stories, settings and feedback Until you delete your account
Which stories were opened each day 30 days, or until you delete your account
Sign-in details (IP address, device type) Until you sign out on that phone, or the sign-in ends
Server records of requests (no names, emails or IP addresses) 7 days
Our sign-in provider's own logs 1 day
A note that a deleted account's sign-in must be refused 30 days after deletion
Backups 30 days, then removed automatically

When you delete your account, we delete everything above straight away, except copies inside backups, which are removed when those backups expire (30 days).

7. Your consent

Before a child profile is made, Varam asks the parent or guardian to confirm that they are an adult, that they are the child's parent or guardian, and that they agree to this policy. We record which version of the policy you agreed to and when.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account (section 8). Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent, and it does not affect anything done before.

8. Your rights and how to use them

You can How
See what we keep Parent Settings → Privacy & data controls → See my data
Correct your child's details Settings → tap your child's profile
Delete everything Parent Settings → Delete account, or email us (see section 13)
Ask a question or make a complaint Write to Varam Support at varam.app@gmail.com
Name someone to act for you if you die or cannot act Write to Varam Support with the person's name and how to reach them

Parent Settings is behind a grown-ups check so that children cannot change these by accident.

We reply to every request within 7 days. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

Varam is made for children, so:

  • A parent or guardian must set up the account and agree first; children cannot sign up on their own.
  • We do not track your child's behaviour for advertising, show them ads, or build marketing profiles.
  • Varam has no chat and no way to contact other users. The only links out of the app are for parents: the privacy policy, the email to Varam Support, and the update screen's download page. Children can only reach the first two through Parent Settings, which is behind the grown-ups check.

10. How we protect information

  • All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Every request is checked so that one account can never see another account's information.
  • Sign-in and downloaded stories are encrypted on the phone.
  • Only the people who run Varam can open the database and the staff console, and every staff action on an account is recorded.
  • Our accounts with the services in section 5 are protected with two-step sign-in.

If information about you or your child is ever exposed, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

11. Changes to this policy

Each version has a number and a date. If we change what we collect or why, the app will show you the new version and ask you to agree again the next time you open it.

12. Contact

Varam Support — varam.app@gmail.com

13. Delete your Varam account

In the app: Parent Settings → Delete account. Everything is deleted straight away.

Without the app (for example if you have uninstalled it): email Varam Support at varam.app@gmail.com from the Google email you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Varam account". We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted: your sign-in, your account, your child's profile, reading progress, saved stories, feedback and the record of stories opened. Copies inside our backups are removed when those backups expire, within 30 days. The record of which policy version you agreed to is deleted with the account.
